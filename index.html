Rolling Forecast for Mac

A macOS app for fiscal-year budgets, monthly actuals, rolling forecast scenarios, analytics, and Excel / zip backup workflows.

  • Account hierarchy with budget, actual, and forecast views
  • Scenario-based rolling forecasts and known expenses
  • Dashboard, analytics, report packs, and export to Excel, PDF, and PowerPoint
  • Optional password protection for each organization
  • Share organizations as read-only copies and choose who can edit them
  • Local zip backup and optional restore from a URL you choose

Rolling Forecast Reports for iPad

A read-only companion that opens the organizations you build on the Mac.

  • Browse reports, charts, and scorecards
  • Present report packs and export them to PDF or PowerPoint
  • Import from Files, iCloud Drive, a link, or the folder your Mac backs up into
  • Never writes back to your Mac data

Help and privacy

Questions or problems? Visit Support. Read how both apps handle your data in the privacy policy.
